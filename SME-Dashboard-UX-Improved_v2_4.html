Ready to begin your curation journey? Use the process below to help you get started.

Step 1

Select a Data Asset

Use the left navigation panel to select a Data Asset. Filter by Curation Status, Data Domain, or Search.

Step 2

View Attribute Details

After selecting a Data Asset, its attributes will appear on the right side of the screen.

Step 3

Edit Functional Names or Descriptions

Use AI suggestions to create a functional name or description. When your edit differs from current values, the field turns brown.

Step 4

Submit Attribute Changes

When ready, click Submit. To save progress and return later, click Save.
